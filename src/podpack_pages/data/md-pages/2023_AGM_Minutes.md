## Brede Steam Amateur Radio Society
### 2023 Annual General Meeting: 1 April, 2023

The meeting was called to order by Daniel Adkin at 12:00.

  1. Present at the meeting: [I don't have the roster, can someone please let me have a copy?]

     Apologies for absence were received from

     - Keith Bird (RSGB Regional Manager)
     - Matt Woods
     - Richard Whatling
     - Rolf

   2. Daniel explained that due to varioius factors, principal among them being the pandemic,
      there were no minutes for the previous AGM, which was help four years previously.

   3. Daniel further explained the the club's chairman had died in the last year,
      and that the Committee had held occasional telephone meetings.
      It had been agreed there should be no charges for the shack until
      the club started meeting again.
      The club had agreed to trade shack rental for keeping the grass down in the field
      behind the shack.

      Although Daniel had kept the club afloat during the pandemic, he stated that
      he did not want it to be thought of as "Daniel's club," and that active
      participation from other members was required to bring the club back to health.
      Various projects were discussed:

      - Web site (Steve)
      - Brickworks (Clive)
      - Grounds maintenance
      - Club logo design
      - Coronation day

   4. Daniel give a short secretary's report, essentially underlining what had already been said.

   5. Antony Turnbull presented a short treasurer's report.
      Little had been happening for three years,
      there had been no income and modest expenditures.
      The meeting approved the accounts presented (see Appendix).

   6. Elections were held, and the following officers were elected:

      - _Chairman_: Philip Tomlins
      - _Secretary_: Steve Holden
      - _Treasurer_: Antony Turnbull
      - _Contests/Events Manager_: Daniel Adkin (with assistance from Matt and Ricky)
      - _QSL Manager_: Mike Wade
      - _Publicity Manager_: Ricky Nye

     Clive Poling was co-opted as Brickworks Coordinator.

   7. Under "Any Other Business" access to the shack was discussed.
      Daniel said that there was a proposal to fit a combination lock,
      but we needed to determine whether doing so would invlidate the club's insurance.
      Until this was resolved the meeting delegated the secretary to have a
      number of additional keys cut and distributed to committee members.

      There being no other competent business the meeting adjourned at approximately 13:30.
